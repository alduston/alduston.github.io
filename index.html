Featured project

End-to-end latent diffusion with CSEM In progress

Latent diffusion usually trains the autoencoder first and then fits a score network to its frozen latents. Component Score Expectation Matching (CSEM) trains them together. It replaces the sampled-noise target of denoising score matching with the analytic score of each encoder posterior. Because that target depends on the encoder's mean and covariance, the diffusion loss also trains the encoder (orange path), without the latent collapse that joint training with the standard denoising loss produces.
